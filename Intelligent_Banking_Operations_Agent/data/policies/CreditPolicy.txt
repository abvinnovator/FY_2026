## SECTION 1: INCOME AND AFFORDABILITY POLICIES

### Policy 1.1 - Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)
The DTI ratio measures the percentage of gross monthly income used to pay monthly debt obligations.

**Calculation**: DTI = (Total Monthly Debt Payments / Gross Monthly Income) × 100

**Thresholds**:
- DTI ≤ 28%: Excellent - Full approval recommended
- DTI 28-36%: Good - Standard approval with monitoring
- DTI 36-43%: Moderate Risk - Requires additional documentation
- DTI 43-50%: High Risk - Requires senior underwriter review
- DTI > 50%: Critical - Automatic decline unless exceptional circumstances
- DTI > 60%: Hard Decline - No exceptions permitted

**Policy Violation Code**: DTI-001 through DTI-006

### Policy 1.2 - FOIR (Fixed Obligations to Income Ratio)
For Indian banking context, FOIR is used alongside DTI.

**Calculation**: FOIR = (All Fixed Monthly Obligations / Net Monthly Income) × 100

**Thresholds**:
- FOIR ≤ 40%: Recommended for approval
- FOIR 40-50%: Conditional approval
- FOIR > 50%: Requires escalation
- FOIR > 65%: Automatic decline

**Policy Violation Code**: FOIR-001 through FOIR-004

### Policy 1.3 - Minimum Income Requirements
- Salaried Applicants: Minimum ₹25,000/month or $3,000/month
- Self-Employed: Minimum ₹35,000/month or $4,500/month with ITR for 2 years
- Credit Card: Minimum income 3x the requested credit limit

**Policy Violation Code**: INC-001 (Below minimum income)

## SECTION 2: CREDIT HISTORY POLICIES

### Policy 2.1 - Delinquency Assessment
Payment history is the strongest predictor of future credit behavior.

**Delinquency Classifications**:
- 30 Days Past Due (DPD-30): Minor delinquency, -10 points
- 60 Days Past Due (DPD-60): Moderate delinquency, -20 points  
- 90+ Days Past Due (DPD-90): Serious delinquency, -35 points
- 180+ Days Past Due: Default status, automatic decline
- Written-off/Charge-off: Hard decline for 7 years
- Bankruptcy: Hard decline for 10 years (Chapter 7) or 7 years (Chapter 13)

**Policy Violation Code**: DEL-001 through DEL-006

### Policy 2.2 - Credit Utilization Ratio
**Calculation**: Utilization = (Current Credit Used / Total Credit Limit) × 100

**Thresholds**:
- Utilization ≤ 30%: Excellent
- Utilization 30-50%: Acceptable
- Utilization 50-75%: Concerning - suggests cash flow issues
- Utilization > 75%: High risk - potential credit stress
- Utilization > 90%: Critical - strong decline indicator

**Policy Violation Code**: UTL-001 through UTL-005

### Policy 2.3 - Credit History Length
- < 6 months: Thin file - limited data for decision
- 6-12 months: New credit user - higher scrutiny
- 1-3 years: Developing credit - standard review
- 3-7 years: Established credit - favorable
- > 7 years: Mature credit - most favorable

**Policy Violation Code**: HIST-001 (Insufficient credit history)

## SECTION 3: LOAN-SPECIFIC POLICIES

### Policy 3.1 - Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV)
For secured loans, LTV determines risk exposure.

**Calculation**: LTV = (Loan Amount / Collateral Value) × 100

**Thresholds by Loan Type**:
- Home Loan: Maximum 80% LTV (90% for first-time buyers)
- Auto Loan: Maximum 85% LTV for new, 70% for used
- Loan Against Property: Maximum 65% LTV
- Gold Loan: Maximum 75% LTV

**Policy Violation Code**: LTV-001 (Exceeds maximum LTV)

### Policy 3.2 - Maximum Loan Amount by Category
- Personal Loan: Maximum 15x monthly income
- Credit Card: Maximum 5x monthly income as limit
- Home Loan: Maximum 60x monthly income
- Auto Loan: Maximum 4x annual income

**Policy Violation Code**: AMT-001 (Exceeds maximum loan amount)

### Policy 3.3 - Requested Limit to Income Ratio
**Calculation**: Limit Ratio = (Requested Credit Limit / Monthly Income) × 100

**Thresholds**:
- Ratio ≤ 25%: Conservative - auto-approve eligible
- Ratio 25-50%: Standard - normal review
- Ratio 50-100%: Aggressive - requires justification
- Ratio > 100%: Excessive - automatic flag for review
- Ratio > 200%: Decline - unrealistic request

**Policy Violation Code**: REQ-001 through REQ-003

## SECTION 4: EMPLOYMENT AND STABILITY POLICIES

### Policy 4.1 - Employment Tenure Requirements
- Current Job: Minimum 6 months in current role
- Industry: Minimum 1 year total work experience
- Self-Employed: Minimum 2 years in current business

**Policy Violation Code**: EMP-001 (Insufficient employment tenure)

### Policy 4.2 - Income Stability Assessment
- Salaried: Consistent income for past 3 months
- Variable Income: Average of past 12 months, exclude spikes
- Commission-Based: Use 70% of average as qualifying income

**Policy Violation Code**: STAB-001 (Unstable income pattern)

---

## SECTION 5: AGE AND DEMOGRAPHIC POLICIES

### Policy 5.1 - Age Requirements
- Minimum Age: 21 years (18 with co-signer)
- Maximum Age at Loan Maturity: 65 years (salaried), 70 years (self-employed)
- Senior Applicants (60+): Require life insurance assignment

**Policy Violation Code**: AGE-001, AGE-002

---

## SECTION 6: SCORING AND DECISION MATRIX

### Policy 6.1 - Credit Score Integration
**CIBIL/FICO Score Mapping**:
- 750+: Excellent - Pre-approved, lowest rates
- 700-749: Good - Standard approval
- 650-699: Fair - Higher rates, additional scrutiny
- 600-649: Poor - Secured products only
- < 600: Very Poor - Decline or require guarantor

**Policy Violation Code**: SCORE-001 (Below minimum score threshold)

### Policy 6.2 - Combined Decision Matrix
Final decision based on weighted factors:
- Credit Score: 30% weight
- DTI Ratio: 25% weight
- Payment History: 20% weight
- Employment Stability: 15% weight
- Credit Utilization: 10% weight

**Automatic Decline Triggers**:
- Any hard decline policy violation
- Combined score < 45/100
- Multiple medium-severity violations (3+)

---

## SECTION 7: EXCEPTION HANDLING

### Policy 7.1 - Override Authority
- Branch Manager: Can override up to 5 points
- Regional Credit Head: Can override up to 10 points
- Chief Credit Officer: Can override up to 20 points
- Board Level: Required for exceptions > 20 points

### Policy 7.2 - Compensating Factors
The following may compensate for marginal violations:
- Significant savings (> 6 months expenses)
- Strong employment history (> 5 years same employer)
- Low utilization on existing credit (< 20%)
- Property ownership (residential)
- Guarantor with excellent credit
